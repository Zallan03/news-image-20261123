## 1. Nepal PM meets Modi in New Delhi — DIPLOMACY

Nepal Prime Minister Balendra Shah holds delegation-level talks with
Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi in New Delhi on Monday, 23
November 2026 — his first bilateral visit abroad since taking office
in March 2026. The two leaders review the full range of India–Nepal
ties (trade, energy, transport, connectivity, infrastructure) and
exchange MoUs on education, health, agriculture, sports, disaster
management and human-resource development. Shah leads a 34-member
delegation, met External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar on Sunday
evening, and calls on President Droupadi Murmu. Modi hosts an
official luncheon; the two PMs address a joint press conference.

## 2. The man behind the Nvidia chip-smuggling web — TECH

The Wall Street Journal names Li Ming, a 52-year-old Chinese national
out on bail in Singapore, as the central operator of a network of
companies smuggling restricted Nvidia AI chips into China. The web
included Megaspeed, the Singapore-based company where Alice Huang —
the businesswoman at the centre of the affair that ended Jensen
Huang's Nvidia leadership — was a managing director. Prosecutors
allege Li bought servers fitted with Nvidia chips, claiming they were
for his local firm Luxuriate Your Life, then shipped them via Malaysia
onward. Megaspeed is suspected of arranging close to $12bn of chips
between March 2024 and June 2025; Nvidia put it on an internal
"no-sell" list in June 2025. The story broke overnight 22–23 November
2026 (WSJ; picked up by The Times the same morning).

## 3. Seoul minister quits over AI exam-cheating chaos — ASIA

South Korean Education Minister Choi Kyo-jin resigns, taking
responsibility for confusion caused by last-minute measures to prevent
AI-assisted cheating in the CSAT college entrance exam (held 19
November). President Lee Jae Myung accepts the resignation within an
hour and orders a comprehensive review of national exam management and
related laws. The row followed the 15 November confession of the top
scorer in a national tax-accountant exam, who admitted using a
miniature camera and AI to cheat; the ministry caught 111 suspected
cheating cases with electronic devices during the CSAT. Reuters
carried the resignation as a 23 November 2026 story.

## 4. Gaza shelling kills two as the truce frays — MIDDLE EAST

Two Palestinians are killed in Israeli shelling in southern Gaza
overnight, reported early Monday 23 November 2026 (bulletin published
2026-11-23T00:10:11Z). It is the latest deadly incident since the
ceasefire that took effect on 10 October 2025: on Saturday 21 November
four Palestinians were killed and 14 wounded across Gaza, including
three in a strike on a food-distribution vehicle in Gaza City. Gaza
health officials count 1,671 Palestinians killed since the truce
began.
